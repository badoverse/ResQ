WHICH AI MODEL SHOULD I PICK?
=============================

All models run on this computer only. Nothing you type is sent to the internet.
Bigger models give better answers, but they are slower and need more memory (RAM).
If you are not sure, pick resq:3b. If the computer has 8 GB of RAM or more, pick resq:7b:
it gives the best answers in an emergency.
The two 1.5b models are too small for emergencies: they can refuse or repeat themselves.


resq:1.5b - "The quick helper"
  Size: 986 MB    Needs: 4 GB RAM or more
  The smallest and fastest model. It works on almost any computer, even an old one.
  Good for: short questions, quick definitions, simple rewording, short translations.
  Not good for: emergencies, long or tricky questions. It can refuse, repeat itself or invent facts.

resq:3b - "The everyday helper"  (start here)
  Size: 1.9 GB    Needs: 6 GB RAM or more
  The best balance of speed and quality for normal use.
  Good for: writing and fixing emails, summaries, explanations, brainstorming,
  translating between common languages, simple coding help.
  Not good for: hard math, or facts that must be exactly right.

resq:7b - "The best helper"  (best for emergencies)
  Size: 4.7 GB    Needs: 8 GB RAM or more
  The strongest general model here. Clearer, more accurate answers, and better Italian.
  Good for: emergency instructions, first aid steps, planning, anything important.
  Not good for: old or low-memory computers. It is slower than resq:3b.

resq-think:1.5b - "The small thinker"
  Size: 1.1 GB    Needs: 4 GB RAM or more
  It thinks out loud before it answers. You see its reasoning first, then the answer.
  Good for: simple puzzles and step-by-step problems on a low-power computer.
  Not good for: emergencies, chatting or writing. It is small, so its reasoning can go wrong.
  Works best in English.

resq-think:7b - "The careful thinker"
  Size: 4.7 GB    Needs: 8 GB RAM or more
  A stronger thinking model. It is slower, because it works the problem out before it answers.
  Good for: math, logic problems, planning, checking an argument, harder code questions.
  Not good for: quick chat. Simple questions get long answers.
  Works best in English.

resq-vision:7b - "The one that can see"
  Size: 6.0 GB    Needs: 10 GB RAM or more
  The only model here that can look at pictures.
  Good for: reading text from a photo or screenshot, describing an image,
  reading tables, timetables, receipts and forms.
  How: in the browser chat, press the "Image" button, pick the photo, type your question, press Enter.
  (In terminal mode: type your question, drag the image file into the window, press Enter.)
  Not good for: slow or low-memory computers. It is the heaviest model here.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - The first answer takes longer, because the model has to load from the USB stick.
  - These models can be wrong and can sound confident when they are wrong. Check important facts.
  - They don't know about recent events, and they can't search the internet.
  - Each chat starts fresh. They don't remember your earlier chats.
  - If the computer becomes very slow or freezes, close the chat and pick a smaller model.
